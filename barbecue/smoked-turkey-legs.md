# Smoked turkey legs

> Makes 12 small turkey drumsticks. Brine 12-18 hours, smoke at 260-275 F to 180 F internal, then finish in a 450 F oven for 5-8 minutes.

Weigh together, including bones, and record the total weight in grams:

- 12 small **turkey drumsticks**

Calculate the pink curing salt by weight, using the equilibrium method for a target of approximately 156 parts per million sodium nitrite:

> Cure #1 (grams) = 0.0025 × (6000 + turkey weight in grams)
>
> For example, if the 12 legs weigh 5000 grams total, use 27.5 grams of Cure #1.
>
> This assumes standard Cure #1 containing 6.25% sodium nitrite.

In a large pot, heat gently, stirring until the salt and sugar dissolve. Avoid a long boil, which can make the sage and rosemary taste bitter:

- 1500 grams **water**
- 300 grams **kosher salt**
- 600 grams **brown sugar**
- 60 grams fresh **poultry herbs** (thyme, sage, rosemary, bay leaves), about three 0.75 ounce packages
- 5 grams **black peppercorns**
- 1 head **garlic**, cloves separated and smashed in their skins

Remove from heat, and add:

- 4500 grams **ice**

Stir in until fully dissolved:

- **Pink curing salt #1** (6.25% sodium nitrite), weighed as calculated above

Keep stirring until the brine is at or below 40 F. Some ice will remain, which is fine.

Completely submerge turkey legs in the brine in a food-safe container.

Refrigerate at 40 F or below for 12-18 hours.

Remove the turkey legs and discard the brine. Rinse thoroughly under cold water, then pat thoroughly dry. Arrange on wire racks over sheet pans, and refrigerate uncovered for 1-4 hours to dry the skin and develop a slightly tacky surface.

Preheat the smoker to 260-275 F with:

- **Oak** or **hickory** wood, optionally blended with **apple** or **cherry** wood

Arrange the legs on the grates with space between them. Smoke until each leg reaches 180 F internal in its thickest portion, avoiding the bone when measuring. Expect approximately 2.5-3.5 hours, depending on size and smoker performance.

Remove the legs at 180 F internal, and rest loosely tented with aluminum foil for 15-20 minutes. Preheat the oven to 450 F during the rest.

Arrange the rested legs on wire racks over sheet pans. Roast at 450 F for 5-8 minutes, until the skin tightens and becomes slightly crisp. Avoid prolonged roasting, which could dry out the meat.

Serve immediately.
