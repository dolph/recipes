# Thanksgiving 2026

> Serves 10 people with plenty of leftovers.
>
> Comparison to [`thanksgiving.md`](thanksgiving.md):
>
> - No cornbread dressing
> - Two turkeys: roast the breasts and thighs, smoke the drumsticks (see [smoked turkey legs](../barbecue/smoked-turkey-legs.md))
> - Break down and dry brine on Tuesday; make gravy and sides on Wednesday
>
> Times on Thursday are counted back from dinner (**T**). For a 4 PM dinner, T-5h is 11 AM.

- **Turkey**: Bon Appétit's [Expertly Spiced and Glazed Roast Turkey](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/expertly-spiced-and-glazed-roast-turkey) (breasts and thighs) + [smoked turkey legs](../barbecue/smoked-turkey-legs.md)
- **Gravy**: Bon Appétit's [Why-Is-It-So-Good Gravy](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/why-is-it-so-good-gravy)
- **Mashed potatoes**: Bon Appétit's [Mashed Potatoes With Crispety Cruncheties](https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/mashed-potatoes-with-crispety-cruncheties)

Schedule:

- **Friday**: Thaw turkeys (if necessary)
- **Tuesday**: Make dry brine mixture, break down turkeys, dry brine breasts and thighs
- **Wednesday**: Cook gravy, cook mashed potatoes and topping, make glaze, brine turkey legs (between T-25h and T-19h)
- **Thursday**: Smoke turkey legs, roast breasts and thighs, finish sides

## Friday

Start defrosting turkeys in refrigerator by Friday evening (24 hours for every 4 pounds, so 3.5 days, before Tuesday):

- 2 14-pound **whole turkeys**

## Tuesday

### Dry brine

> Sized for about 7.5 kilograms of breasts and thighs (roughly 1% salt).

Combine in a medium bowl:

- 24 grams (3.5 tablespoons) whole **black peppercorns**, very finely ground
- 14 grams (3.5 tablespoons) whole **pink peppercorns**, very finely ground
- 70 grams **Morton kosher salt**
- 33 grams (3.5 tablespoons) **garlic powder** (contains fructans, which may trigger intolerance)
- 25 grams (3.5 tablespoons) **onion powder** (contains fructans, which may trigger intolerance)
- 12 grams (1.75 tablespoons) **smoked paprika**
- 21 grams (1.75 tablespoons) **light brown sugar**

### Break down turkeys

Separate each turkey into breast (keep whole, on the bone, as a crown), leg quarters, wings, and backbone. Split each leg quarter at the joint into a drumstick and a thigh.

- Turkeys

Refrigerate the drumsticks, covered, for brining on Wednesday.

Refrigerate the wings, backbones, necks, hearts, and gizzards, covered, for gravy on Wednesday. Discard the livers or save them for another use.

### Dry brine breasts and thighs

Place one breast crown and two thighs on each of two wire racks set in rimmed half sheet pans (triple lined with aluminum foil if roasting in the same pans on Thanksgiving). Apply dry brine mixture evenly over all pieces, and refrigerate uncovered until Thursday (about 48 hours).

- Turkey breasts
- Turkey thighs
- Dry brine mixture

## Wednesday

Oven order today: gravy parts at 450 F, then potatoes at 425 F, then topping at 350 F.

### Beurre manié

With gloved fingers (or a fork), mash together in a 24-ounce container until homogeneous:

- 55 grams **flour**
- 56 grams (4 tablespoons) **butter**, room temperature

Set aside for gravy.

### Gravy

Preheat oven to 450 F.

In a large roasting pan, toss turkey parts with oil until coated, spreading them in as close to a single layer as possible:

- Wings, backbones, and necks from both turkeys (about 6 pounds), broken down into 1" pieces
- 55 grams (4 tablespoons) **olive oil**

Roast, tossing halfway through, until golden brown and starting to crisp (about 25–35 minutes).

Remove roasting pan from oven and add, tossing to coat with oil:

- 4 heads of **garlic**, cut in half crosswise
- 4 large **shallots**, unpeeled, halved
- 115 grams **ginger** (about an 8" piece), scrubbed, thickly sliced
- 225 grams **baby bella, shiitake, or white button mushrooms**, torn into large pieces
- 16 grams **black peppercorns**, lightly crushed
- 120 grams **olive oil**

Return to oven and roast, tossing halfway through, until poultry is deeply browned and crisp and vegetables are browned (about 30–40 minutes).

Remove roasting pan from oven, and lower oven to 425 F for the potatoes. Transfer solids to a 7-quart stock pot.

Set roasting pan over two burners on medium-high heat. Add sherry and cook, stirring and scraping up any browned bits with a wooden spoon, until sherry is almost completely reduced (about 2 minutes):

- 120 grams (0.5 cup) **amontillado sherry**

Scrape the deglazed bits into the stock pot, add the remaining ingredients, and bring to a low simmer over medium-high heat:

- Reserved hearts and gizzards
- 3 quarts **chicken stock**, warmed
- 30 grams **parsley**, including stems

Reduce heat and simmer gently, skimming fat from surface occasionally and turning wings so both sides get a chance to be submerged, until liquid is flavorful, mahogany in color, and reduced by about one-third (about 30–40 minutes).

Strain through a fat separator, discarding fat and solids. Rinse pot, return broth to pot, and bring to a simmer.

Add and whisk constantly until completely incorporated:

- Beurre manié

Simmer, whisking often and skimming any foam from surface, until gravy is thick enough to coat a spoon (about 6–8 minutes).

Cool, cover, and refrigerate.

### Roast potatoes

Once the oven is at 425 F, place potatoes on a rimmed baking sheet or in a large baking dish and cover tightly with foil. Roast until very tender (about 80–95 minutes) and internal temperature reaches 210 F.

- 5 pounds **Yukon Gold potatoes**, scrubbed

### Garlic mixture

Meanwhile, place in a large saucepan:

- 336 grams (24 tablespoons) **butter**
- 1 large head of **garlic**, halved crosswise
- 6 sprigs **thyme**
- 10 grams **black peppercorns**, lightly crushed
- 30 grams **kosher salt**
- 1050 grams (5 cups) **whole milk**
- Zest of 1 large **lemon** (in large strips)

Bring mixture to a simmer over medium-high heat, stirring occasionally. Immediately remove from heat, cover, and let sit at least 30 minutes.

### Mashed potatoes

When potatoes are done, lower oven to 350 F for the topping. Remove skins while potatoes are very hot (hold with a kitchen towel), add to a 6- to 8-quart pot, and mash immediately.

Return garlic mixture to a simmer, then strain into pot with potatoes, discarding solids.

Set potato mixture over medium heat and whisk until liquid is incorporated and potatoes are very smooth and bubbling. Taste and season with more salt if needed.

Transfer to a 13x9" baking dish, cover, and refrigerate.

### Mashed potatoes topping

Pulse in a food processor until coarse crumbs form (about 1 minute):

- 100 grams **country-style bread** (about two 1/2"-thick slices), torn into 1" pieces

Add to breadcrumbs, and pulse just once or twice to combine:

- 70 grams (about 2.5 cups) **kettle-style potato chips** (such as Cape Cod)

Heat in a large ovenproof skillet over medium-low heat, swirling, until butter is foaming and garlic is fragrant and just golden (about 4 minutes), then remove and discard garlic:

- 70 grams (5 tablespoons) **butter**
- 5 cloves of **garlic**, smashed

Add to the garlic butter over medium heat:

- Breadcrumb mixture
- 3 grams kosher salt
- 1 gram freshly ground black pepper

Cook, stirring, until mixture is coated in butter and fragrant, about 1 minute.

Transfer skillet to 350 F oven and bake, tossing every 5 minutes, until dark golden brown (about 15–20 minutes).

Meanwhile, combine in a small bowl:

- 4 sprigs **thyme**, leaves picked
- 3 grams (1 1/4 teaspoons) **hot smoked Spanish paprika**
- Zest of 1 large **lemon**, finely grated

Remove crumb mixture from oven and immediately toss in thyme mixture. Taste and season with more salt if needed. Let topping cool in pan.

Transfer to a 32-ounce container and refrigerate.

### Turkey glaze

Cook in a small saucepan over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until sugar is dissolved and glaze thickens slightly (it should just barely coat a spoon, about 10-12 minutes).

- 10 grams fresh **poultry herbs** (sage, rosemary, bay leaves, and/or thyme)
- 4 cloves **garlic**, crushed
- 2 2x1" strips **orange** zest
- 80 grams (~1/3 cup) **soy sauce**
- 80 grams (~1/3 cup) **red wine vinegar**
- 70 grams (~1/3 cup) **brown sugar**

Remove glaze from heat, discard solids, and refrigerate.

### Brine turkey legs

> Start between T-25h and T-19h, so the legs get 12–18 hours before coming out at T-7h. For a 4 PM dinner, that's between 3 PM and 9 PM tonight. This is one batch of the [smoked turkey legs](../barbecue/smoked-turkey-legs.md) brine.

Weigh together, including bones, and record the total weight in grams:

- Drumsticks from both turkeys
- 9 small **turkey drumsticks**

Calculate the pink curing salt by weight, using the equilibrium method for a target of approximately 156 parts per million sodium nitrite:

> Cure #1 (grams) = 0.0025 × (6000 + turkey weight in grams)
>
> For example, if the legs weigh 5000 grams total, use 27.5 grams of Cure #1.

In a large pot, heat gently, stirring until the salt and sugar dissolve:

- 1500 grams **water**
- 300 grams **kosher salt**
- 600 grams **brown sugar**
- 20 grams fresh **poultry herbs** (thyme, sage, rosemary, bay leaves)
- 5 grams **black peppercorns**
- 9 cloves **garlic**, smashed

Remove from heat, and add:

- 4500 grams **ice**

Stir in until fully dissolved:

- **Pink curing salt #1** (6.25% sodium nitrite), weighed as calculated above

Keep stirring until the brine is at or below 40 F. Some ice will remain, which is fine.

Completely submerge the legs in the brine in a 16-quart food-safe container, and refrigerate at 40 F or below.

## Thursday

### Prep

#### Flavor enhancing slurry

Whisk together in a small bowl and set aside:

- 2 teaspoons **sherry vinegar** or **red wine vinegar**
- 1 teaspoon **MSG**

#### Minced parsley

- 20 grams (about 1/3 cup) **parsley**, minced

### Smoke turkey legs

**T-7h**: Remove the legs and discard the brine. Rinse thoroughly under cold water, then pat thoroughly dry. Arrange on wire racks over sheet pans, and refrigerate uncovered to dry the skin and develop a slightly tacky surface.

- Brined turkey legs

**T-5.5h**: Preheat the smoker to 260-275 F with:

- **Oak** or **hickory** wood, optionally blended with **apple** or **cherry** wood

**T-5h**: Arrange the legs on the grates with space between them. Smoke until each piece reaches 180 F internal in its thickest portion, avoiding the bone when measuring (about 2.5–3.5 hours; the small drumsticks will finish first).

As each piece reaches 180 F, wrap it in aluminum foil and hold it in a dry cooler lined with towels until serving.

### Roast turkey breasts and thighs

**T-3.5h**: Rest breasts and thighs at room temperature for 1 hour.

**T-2.5h**: Place two racks in the middle of the oven, with enough room for a baking sheet of breast and thighs on each; preheat to 425 F.

Rub breasts and thighs:

- 80 grams (6 tablespoons) **avocado oil**

Pour into each baking sheet:

- 240 grams (1 cup) **water**

Roast until skin is mostly golden brown (about 20-25 minutes).

**T-2h**: Reduce oven temperature to 300 F and continue to roast, swapping racks and rotating baking sheets halfway through, brushing with glaze every 20 minutes, and adding more water as needed to maintain liquid in baking sheets, until the breasts register 150 F and the thighs register 170 F (50-80 minutes longer; two loaded sheets in one oven tend toward the long end). Remove each piece as it reaches temperature.

- Turkey glaze, warmed

**About T-1h**: Once everything is out of the oven, tent with foil, and rest 30-60 minutes before carving. Reserve drippings for gravy. Raise oven to 450 F for the turkey legs.

### Finish gravy

**T-1.5h**: Return to a gentle simmer, cover, and hold until turkey comes out of the oven:

- Gravy

**About T-1h**: Add and whisk to combine:

- Turkey drippings

Remove from heat and whisk in:

- Flavor enhancing slurry

Season to taste:

- Kosher salt
- Black pepper

### Finish mashed potatoes

**T-30m**: Transfer to a 6- to 8-quart pot and reheat over medium, whisking constantly and loosening with more milk if needed:

- Mashed potatoes
- **Whole milk**, as needed

Add:

- Minced parsley

Transfer mashed potatoes to a large bowl and sprinkle topping over just before serving.

- Mashed potatoes topping

### Finish turkey legs

**T-15m**: Unwrap the legs and arrange on wire racks over sheet pans. Roast at 450 F for 5-8 minutes, until the skin tightens and becomes slightly crisp.

- Smoked turkey legs

Serve immediately.
