# Creamy Parmesan & Garlic Pasta

A rich, silky white Parmesan cream sauce with mellow garlic, butter, and a
little lemon to balance the richness. Serve with seared shrimp or chicken
breast. Mild enough for kids, but flavorful enough to stand on its own.

Serves 4–5. Approximately 35 minutes, excluding optional dry-brining.

## Protein

Choose one of the following proteins. Cook it first, then use the same
pan to prepare the sauce.

### Garlic butter shrimp

Pat the shrimp dry and season evenly:

- 600 g **large shrimp**, peeled and deveined
- 5 g salt
- 1 g **sweet paprika**

Heat a large stainless steel sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add:

- 15 g **unsalted butter**
- 10 g **olive oil**

Sear the shrimp in a single layer, approximately 90 seconds per side,
until opaque and cooked through to 145°F. Work in batches if necessary.

Finish with:

- 5 g **fresh lemon juice**

Transfer to a plate and keep warm. Do not clean the pan.

### Golden chicken breast

Butterfly the chicken breasts into cutlets approximately 1/2 inch thick.
Season evenly:

- 650 g **boneless, skinless chicken breast**
- 7 g salt
- 1 g **garlic powder**
- 1 g freshly ground black pepper

For best results, dry-brine uncovered in the refrigerator for 4–12 hours.

Heat a large stainless steel sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add:

- 15 g **olive oil**

Sear the chicken until golden brown on both sides and the thickest
portion reaches 165°F.

Transfer to a cutting board and rest for 5 minutes before slicing
across the grain. Reserve any accumulated juices.

Do not clean the pan.

## Garlic butter

Reduce the heat to medium-low. Add to the pan:

- 45 g **unsalted butter**
- 20 g **fresh garlic**, finely minced or grated

Cook gently for 3–5 minutes, stirring frequently, until the garlic
softens and becomes fragrant. Avoid browning the garlic.

Stir in and cook for another 30 seconds:

- 1 g **dried thyme**
- 0.5 g **freshly grated nutmeg** (optional)
- 0.5 g **MSG**

## Parmesan cream sauce

Reduce the heat to low and gradually stir in:

- 240 g **heavy cream**
- 180 g **whole milk**
- 60 g **low-sodium chicken stock** (or seafood stock for shrimp)

Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring
occasionally, until slightly reduced.

Remove from the heat and gradually whisk in, one handful at a time:

- 100 g **Parmigiano Reggiano**, freshly grated
- 35 g **low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella**, finely shredded

Stir until the cheese is completely melted and the sauce is smooth.
Avoid boiling after adding the cheese.

Finish with:

- 5 g **fresh lemon juice**
- 1 g **fresh lemon zest**, finely grated (optional)
- 1 g **ground white pepper**
- Salt, to taste

The sauce should be slightly thicker than heavy cream at this stage.
It will thicken further as it cools and when combined with the pasta.

## Pasta

Bring 3 liters of water to a boil and add:

- 36 g salt
- 400 g **linguine, fettuccine, penne, or mezze rigatoni**

Cook until approximately 1 minute shy of al dente.

Reserve at least 250 mL of the starchy cooking water before draining.

Return the sauce to low heat and add the drained pasta.

Add 60 mL reserved pasta water and toss vigorously for 1–2 minutes,
until the sauce becomes glossy and clings evenly to the pasta.

Add additional pasta water, 15–30 mL at a time, until the sauce
reaches the desired consistency.

The finished sauce should flow easily and coat the pasta without
forming thick clumps.

## Serve

Divide the pasta among warmed bowls. Arrange the shrimp or sliced
chicken over the pasta. For chicken, incorporate any reserved resting
juices into the sauce before serving.

Finish with:

- 15 g **Parmigiano Reggiano**, finely grated
- 5 g **fresh flat-leaf parsley**, finely chopped (optional)
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Serve immediately.

## Variations

### Lighter cream sauce

Replace 100 g heavy cream with 100 g additional whole milk. The
resulting sauce will be less rich and slightly less stable when reheated.

### Richer Alfredo-style sauce

Omit the mozzarella and stock. Increase the Parmesan to 130 g and
the butter to 65 g. The sauce will be richer and more intensely cheesy,
but more susceptible to separating if overheated.

### White miso variation

Whisk 10 g **white miso** into the warm cream before adding the cheese.
Reduce the final salt accordingly. The miso adds subtle savory depth
without substantially changing the appearance or character of the sauce.
